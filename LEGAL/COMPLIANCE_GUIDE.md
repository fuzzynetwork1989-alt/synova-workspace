## 📋 Regulatory Compliance Framework

SYNOVA NEXUS is designed to comply with major global data protection and technology regulations. This guide outlines the compliance framework and requirements for operating in various jurisdictions.

## 🌍 Applicable Regulations

### **Data Protection Laws**
- **GDPR** (EU): General Data Protection Regulation
- **CCPA** (California): Consumer Privacy Act
- **PIPEDA** (UK): UK Data Protection Act
- **LGPD** (Brazil): Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados
- **PDPA** (Philippines): Personal Data Protection Act
- **APPI** (Australia): Privacy Act
- **DPDPA** (South Africa): Protection of Personal Information Act

### **Security Standards**
- **SOC 2 Type II**: Organizational Security and Controls
- **ISO 27001**: Information Security Management
- **ISO 27001**: IT Service Management
- **PCI DSS**: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard
- **HIPAA**: Healthcare Information Privacy and Portability

### **Industry Standards**
- **OWASP**: Open Web Application Security Project
- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework**: Comprehensive security controls
- **CIS Controls**: Critical Infrastructure Protection
- **FISMA**: Financial Services Modernization Act

## 🔒 Compliance Requirements

### **Data Protection**
- **Privacy by Design**: Privacy integrated into system architecture
- **Data Minimization**: Collect only necessary data for service improvement
- **User Consent**: Explicit consent for data processing activities
- **Data Portability**: Users can export and transfer their data
- **Right to be Forgotten**: Complete data deletion upon request
- **Breach Notification**: 72-hour notification requirement
- **Data Retention**: Defined retention periods by data category

### **Security Controls**
- **Access Management**: Role-based access with audit trails
- **Encryption Standards**: AES-256 for data at rest, TLS 1.3 for transit
- **Authentication**: Multi-factor authentication with SSO support
- **Session Security**: Secure session handling with automatic timeout
- **API Security**: Rate limiting, input validation, authentication

### **Incident Response**
- **Detection**: Automated security monitoring and threat detection
- **Assessment**: Rapid triage and impact analysis
- **Containment**: Isolate affected systems
- **Eradication**: Remove threats and implement patches
- **Recovery**: Restore services and improve defenses
- **Communication**: Notify users and authorities as required

## 🌐 International Operations

### **Multi-Jurisdiction Compliance**
- **Export Controls**: Compliance with export control laws
- **Data Localization**: Adaptation for local privacy laws
- **Sanctions Screening**: Compliance with international sanctions lists
- **Consumer Protection**: Adherence to consumer protection regulations
