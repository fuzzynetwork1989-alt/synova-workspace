# SYNOVA NEXUS - Privacy Policy

**Version**: 1.0  
**Last Updated**: 2024-01-19  
**Status**: Template - Requires Legal Review

## 🔒 Privacy Commitment

SYNOVA NEXUS ("the System") is committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring compliance with global data protection regulations. This policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect user data across all SYNOVA NEXUS services.

## 📋 Data Collection

### **Personal Data We Collect**
- **Account Information**: Name, email, profile details (optional)
- **Usage Data**: Feature usage, interaction patterns, performance metrics
- **Communication Data**: Chat history, support tickets, feedback
- **Technical Data**: Device information, browser type, IP address
- **Payment Data**: Billing information, subscription details (if applicable)

### **Data We Do NOT Collect**
- **Sensitive Personal Information**: Social security numbers, financial details
- **Biometric Data**: Fingerprints, facial recognition, voice patterns
- **Location Data**: Precise GPS coordinates, movement patterns
- **Contact Lists**: Personal address books, call logs

## 🛡️ Data Usage & Storage

### **Purpose Limitation**
We collect and process data only for:
- Providing and improving SYNOVA NEXUS services
- Personalizing user experience
- Ensuring system security and performance
- Providing customer support

### **Data Retention**
- **Chat History**: 90 days for free users, 365 days for premium
- **Analytics Data**: 13 months for trend analysis
- **Error Logs**: 30 days for debugging, then anonymized
- **Account Data**: Until account deletion, per user request

### **Data Security**
- **Encryption**: All data encrypted at rest and in transit
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access with audit trails
- **Authentication**: Multi-factor authentication available
- **Regular Backups**: Daily encrypted backups with geographic distribution

## 🌍 User Rights

### **Data Access Rights**
- **View**: Access to personal data through secure dashboard
- **Correction**: Ability to update or delete personal information
- **Export**: Download personal data in machine-readable formats
- **Portability**: Request data transfer to other services

### **Data Deletion**
- **Right to be Forgotten**: Complete data deletion upon request
- **Automatic Cleanup**: Remove data after account inactivity
- **Compliance**: Follow data retention laws by jurisdiction

## 🌐 Third-Party Sharing

### **When We Share Data**
We may share user data with trusted third parties for:
- **Service Providers**: Cloud infrastructure, analytics services
- **Business Partners**: Integration partners for enhanced features
- **Legal Requirements**: Court orders, regulatory compliance
- **Security Vendors**: Security auditing and penetration testing

### **Data Protection Standards**
- **Contractual Agreements**: Data processing agreements with all vendors
- **Security Audits**: Regular security assessments of third parties
- **Minimum Standards**: All vendors must meet or exceed our privacy standards
- **User Consent**: Explicit consent required for data sharing

## 🚨 Incident Response

### **Data Breach Notification**
In the unlikely event of a data breach:
- **Notification Timeline**: Within 72 hours of discovery
- **Content**: What data was affected and how
- **Mitigation**: Steps taken to protect affected users
- **Regulatory**: Notification to relevant authorities as required
- **Support**: Dedicated support channel for affected users

## 📊 Compliance & Governance

### **Regulatory Compliance**
- **GDPR**: Full compliance for EU users
- **CCPA**: Compliance with California Consumer Privacy Act
- **PIPEDA**: Compliance with UK data protection laws
- **Industry Standards**: ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II compliance

### **Internal Governance**
- **Privacy Team**: Dedicated privacy oversight team
- **Regular Audits**: Quarterly privacy and security assessments
- **Employee Training**: Mandatory privacy and data protection training
- **Policy Reviews**: Annual policy review and updates

## 🔒 User Controls

### **Privacy Settings**
Users can control:
- **Data Collection Preferences**: Opt-in/opt-out for specific data types
- **Communication Preferences**: Email notifications, in-app messaging
- **Privacy Levels**: Basic, enhanced, maximum privacy controls
- **Ad Settings**: Personalization based on privacy preferences
- **Account Deletion**: Self-service account deletion and data export
